# OSZICAR electronic rows, summaries, and step numbering
Source: https://vasp.at/wiki/index.php/OSZICAR
Keywords: OSZICAR, step numbering, step index, electronic row, energy summary, step alignment
Reviewed: 2026-10-26

Official-source summary: OSZICAR records current step and iteration information. In an electronic row, N is the electronic step number, E is the current free energy, dE is the free-energy change between consecutive electronic steps, and d eps is the band-energy change. In the subsequent summary, F is the total free energy and E0 is the energy extrapolated to zero smearing. The meaning of the summary's d E depends on the calculation mode and must not be equated directly with the electronic row's dE. Molecular dynamics summaries use a different format.

Project policy: The comparison tool first checks the last retained ionic step numbers. It compares electronic step numbers only when the ionic numbers agree and the OSZICAR electronic row and following summary are in a valid order. This is VASP-Copilot's comparison policy, not a cross-file alignment algorithm supplied by the official documentation. last_energy_summary=null means only that the parser retained no usable summary; it does not establish that no summary ever appeared in the complete file. Without a summary step number, alignment remains unknown even if the two electronic step numbers match. Matching numbers prove neither shared run identity nor convergence, and a missing summary does not prove calculation failure. File observations must cite actual tool evidence; reference material cannot supply missing step numbers for this run.
